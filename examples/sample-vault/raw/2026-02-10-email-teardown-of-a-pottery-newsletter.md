The studio sends one email per kiln firing, not per week. Each email shows three finished pieces, the firing date and a fixed drop time. Pieces sell out within an hour because the list knows the drop is small and dated.

Subject lines name the glaze, not the studio. Open rates stayed flat when the studio tried weekly digests, so they went back to one email per firing.
