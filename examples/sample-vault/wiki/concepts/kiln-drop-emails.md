# Kiln drop emails

Send one email per firing with three finished pieces and a fixed drop time ([[sources/pottery-newsletter-teardown]]). Name the glaze in the subject line. Related: [[entities/kiln-supply-co]] for firing dates, [[concepts/drop-scarcity]].
